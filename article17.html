The Land of Psyche

By: Malak Sinan

In my land of psyche, a forest of wires
And a sky so grey
Within the forest lied the haunting beasts
within the beasts lied the lust for a prey
Emerged midst the grey land
a deer the color of shear
Sang tunes of hope and cheer
a form to pray
Oh what beautiful words you lay
Don’t you know my child?
That is no place to pray
Not even your tender warmth can make the grey decay
All that is here is doomed to fray
Past the wire trees ran the beautiful stray
The eyes of the beasts
shined something to betray
In the dead of night she serenely lay
Pure innocence to slay
Fangs sunk into her skin
Her shining light grew dim
blood covered mouth formed a grin
And torn her limb from limb
In my land of psyche
The grey turned red
A curse was spread
And With sorrow and dread
Tears were shed
