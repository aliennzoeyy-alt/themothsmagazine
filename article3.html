Webs of Poison

By: Malak Sinan

I need to sleep
Six feet underground
For every sorrow to me is bound
For every face of mutilation in me ,
Is found

Webs of poison
Of my blighted creation woven
Grimy words remained unspoken
Blight spreads upon each heart i touch,
Irreparable being born broken

My webs of poison
How cruel of you ?
To engulf the valley where i dwelled
To poison each flower i had held
The thousand Tears i had to shed
To learn at last,
that an unyielding tree
Cannot, cannot bend .
